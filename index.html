OUR STORY

A Destination Management Company Built in Johannesburg

Malala-Tau Holdings began in 2003 as a small, proudly South African, BEE-owned tour operator working the routes we knew best — Soweto, the Cradle of Humankind, and the reserves within a day's drive of Johannesburg.

Two decades on we operate as a full destination management company. We handle ground operations for inbound tour operators, corporate and government clients, schools and universities, and travellers arriving from all over the world. Safaris, cultural journeys, airport transfers and conference logistics all run out of the same office, with the same people accountable for them.

The training academy came out of a simple observation: the industry is short of good guides, and the people best placed to become them often cannot afford the route in. So we built it ourselves.
